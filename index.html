ABOUT THE PROGRAM

A more inclusive digital future starts with access.

Digital Access is a learning initiative designed to make everyday technology easier to understand and use for persons with disabilities. We focus on useful skills, accessible tools, and the confidence to take part in digital life.

Whether someone is new to smartphones or wants to use online services more independently, learning should be welcoming, flexible, and respectful of individual needs.

THE LEARNING JOURNEY

Small steps. Meaningful digital skills.

Explore the core topics in our digital awareness program.

▣01 / BASICS

Digital foundations

Get comfortable with smartphones, computers, internet connections, and useful everyday apps.

  • Device basics
  • Internet and email
  • Video calls and messaging
⌘02 / ACCESS

Accessible technology

Discover built-in features and assistive tools that can make devices easier to use.

  • Screen readers and magnification
  • Captions and voice typing
  • Keyboard and switch access
♢03 / SAFETY

Online safety

Learn how to protect personal information and recognize suspicious messages or websites.

  • Strong passwords
  • Scam and phishing awareness
  • Privacy and digital respect
↗04 / OPPORTUNITY

Access services online

Use digital platforms to explore learning, employment, community, and public services.

  • Online learning
  • Accessible job search
  • Finding trusted services

ACCESSIBILITY IS A RIGHT

Different needs.
Equal opportunity.

Accessible technology gives more people the freedom to learn, communicate, create, and participate. The best tool is the one that works for the individual.

Explore useful tools →
◉

For people with visual disabilities

Screen readers, magnification, contrast settings, and text-to-speech.

▤

For people with hearing disabilities

Live captions, transcripts, visual alerts, and text-based communication.

⌨

For people with mobility disabilities

Voice control, keyboard navigation, switch access, and alternative input devices.

◌

For people with cognitive disabilities

Plain language, predictable layouts, reminders, and distraction-reducing settings.
